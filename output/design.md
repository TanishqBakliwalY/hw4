# Campus Customs: Creative Design (Problem 10)

**Goal:** make the site feel like a real Yale campus storefront, not a template, so shoppers stay longer and buy.

| Area | What we changed | Why it helps shoppers stay and buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Fonts & hierarchy** | Three clear levels: **Graduate** (varsity block letters) for the brand, hero and section titles; **Georgia serif** for product names; **Inter** for prices, buttons and body text. Section titles get a blue-and-gold varsity underline stripe. | Looks like a college athletics shop at first glance, which builds trust. The eye goes headline → product → price. |
| **Colour & texture** | Yale blue `#00356b` + white, with **gold** `#b4975a` as the accent (eyebrows, "Shop game-day gear" button, pennant stripes, Dan's collar tag). A faint felt-dot texture on the page background, and diagonal varsity stripes in the hero. | Gold marks the actions that matter. Texture makes the page feel like fabric and merch instead of a flat web page. |
| **"The Game" hero** | The Home hero is now **"Beat Harvard. Look good doing it."**, with a live **countdown** (days/hrs/min/sec) to the 142nd Game (**Sat, Nov 21, 2026, Fenway Park**). A felt **YALE pennant** waves on a pole, Handsome Dan bobs beside it, and there are two calls to action: **Shop game-day gear** and **Ask Handsome Dan**. | A real date gives a **reason to buy now** (urgency). The game-day theme points straight to relevant gear (the Yale vs Harvard tee, football tees). |
| **Pennants & patches** | Home "Shop by category" tiles are now **felt pennants** (alternating navy and gold) that tilt on hover. Prices are **stitched patches** (navy tag with a dashed "stitch" outline). | Familiar campus objects (pennants, embroidered patches) reinforce the brand and draw the eye to categories and prices. |
| **Store ticker** | A scrolling ticker in the top bar (pauses on hover): The Game date · printed on Broadway since 1973 · 30-day returns for unworn items · officially licensed. | Trust signals and the news hook are always visible without taking space. |
| **Product cards & motion** | Cards **fade in in sequence** as you scroll. On hover the image zooms slightly, a **"sizes in stock" strip** slides up (sold-out sizes struck through) and a "View →" cue appears. **Badges** come from live stock: "Sold out", or a pulsing **"Only S & XXL left"**. | Shoppers see availability **before** clicking, so fewer dead ends. Scarcity badges create gentle urgency. Motion makes browsing feel responsive. |
| **Product page** | **Hover-to-zoom** image (follows the cursor, 1.9×) so you can check prints and embroidery. The size table became a **size picker** that shows live stock under each size ("In stock" / "2 left" / crossed-out "Sold out"). Choosing a sold-out size offers **"Find me something similar in L"**, which asks Dan, who uses the alternatives tool. | Size is the last step before buying. Seeing stock per size with one tap, and getting an instant in-stock alternative when it's sold out, saves the sale. |
| **Handsome Dan chat** | An **original illustrated bulldog** (inline SVG we drew, so no photo-rights issues) is the chat launcher, and every few seconds he "peeks" (tilts). Once per visit he shows the bubble *"Woof! Need help finding your size?"*. The chat header reads **Handsome Dan · Campus Customs bulldog** with an online dot and a gold border. Dan's avatar sits next to each reply, the typing dots are **🐾 paw prints**, and the greeting is *"Woof! I'm Handsome Dan…"*. "Ask Handsome Dan" buttons appear in the hero and on product pages. | Yale's mascot is instantly recognisable and friendly, so more shoppers open the chat. The chat is where sizes get checked and sold-out items get swapped, which drives sales. |
| **Light persona (agent)** | A short prompt section lets Dan add **at most one** bulldog flourish per reply ("Bulldog-approved!"), with **none** in refusals, safety or policy answers, or sold-out answers. Accuracy rules, tools and the page cards are unchanged. | Personality without hurting accuracy. Verified: a game-day outfit question ended with "Bulldog-approved!", and a sold-out question got a plain, clear answer with alternatives. |
| **Accessibility & restraint** | All motion (ticker, pennant, Dan, pulses, reveal, zoom) is switched off for shoppers with **prefers-reduced-motion** set. Decorative Dan images are hidden from screen readers (the text next to them already names him). Cards reveal on a 1.5s timer even if the scroll observer never fires. | Delight never blocks anyone from shopping, and products can't get stuck hidden. |

**Verified in the running app (1280×800):**
- **Home:** the Graduate font loaded and the countdown ticks (46 days 15 hrs → … at test time).
- **Gold accents:** the pennant, ticker and Dan launcher with his peek bubble all render.
- **Pierson Crewneck page:** the size picker shows XS In stock / S 2 left / M In stock / **L Sold out** / **XL Sold out** / XXL 2 left (matches the database). Choosing L led to "Find me something similar in L", then Dan replied with Davenport (12 left), Saybrook (2) and Jonathan Edwards (8), shown as cards. Hover-zoom scales to 1.9×.
- **Chat:** the Dan header, avatar bubbles and 🐾 typing indicator display correctly.
